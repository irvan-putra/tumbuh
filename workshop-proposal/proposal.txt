Tumbuh workshop - cohort one
Turn your skill into a real offer in two weeks.
A small, hands-on online workshop for Indonesian developers and creators. In two live sessions you build one concrete offer, set a price, and send your first pitch to a real potential customer. We promise the work gets done - not that someone will buy. That answer comes from the market, and finding it out is the point.

Format

Price
Rp200k early bird, Rp250k regular
Refund
Full refund if you leave after session one unconvinced
How the two weeks go
1
Session one: inventory your skills, pick the one with a likely buyer, define the smallest sellable version of it.
2
The week between: price it and draft your one-page pitch, with feedback in the group chat.
3
Session two: send your first real pitch to a real potential customer, with the group watching and helping.
Honest answers
Will I make money?
Maybe, maybe not - no one honest can promise that. What you are guaranteed is the output: an offer, a price, and a real pitch sent. Whether anyone buys is the market's answer, and you will have it within two weeks instead of wondering for a year.
Why is the first cohort cheap?
Because it is the first one. You help shape it, and your feedback decides what the next cohort looks like.
Who is this not for?
Anyone looking for passive income, crypto signals, or a guaranteed salary. This is two weeks of real work on your own offer.
Save one of the 20 seats.
Cohort one runs as soon as 10 seats are paid. Full refund if session one does not convince you.
