Can Tumbuh start from zero?

Historical proposal - inputs dated 24 Oct 2026

Possible with time instead of cash, assuming you already have a laptop, internet and free accounts. Seven models below, in two families - selling your hours, and building something once that sells without you in the room - each with cash cost, two kinds of break-even, and an explicit scenario. Your time is valued at Rp200k per hour - a valuation assumption, not a market rate. The source draft recorded platform-fee checks dated 24 Oct 2026; your own bank's transfer fees are unverified. Every sales number is a scenario - no paying customers have been observed yet, which says nothing about whether any would buy.

The short answer

Zero cash works two ways. Sell your hours - workshop, mentoring, service - and payment should arrive sooner, but the hours repeat with every sale. Or build a product once - playbook, software tool, subscription app - and the first payment takes longer, but later copies need much less work per sale. Both are unproven hypotheses until someone pays.

With near-zero cash in, cash ROI is undefined - the real input is your time. So each model shows a cash break-even and an economic break-even that counts your hours at Rp200k each. A scenario that sells less than the economic break-even loses money once your time is counted, and this page says so openly instead of hiding it in gross revenue. Consultation is not the only route. Shorter time to first payment is a hypothesis, not observed evidence.

The seven models

1. Pre-sold workshop: from skill to sellable offer

Cash to start
Near Rp 0 - assumes laptop, internet, Google Meet, Google Forms, manual bank transfer
Platform fees
None when collecting by manual transfer; your own bank's transfer fees, if any, are unverified - treat as a small sensitivity
Scope and time
2 sessions x 2 hours live, max 20 seats; ~20h total: 10h prep, 4h selling, 4h live, 2h support and refunds
Cash break-even
The first paid seat, because cash costs are near zero
Economic break-even
20 seats at Rp200k, or 16 at Rp250k - 20h x Rp200k = Rp4.0jt of imputed labor
Scenario: 15 seats at Rp200k
Rp3.0jt cash in, minus Rp4.0jt labor = -Rp1.0jt, before tax and refunds
Scenario: full house at Rp250k
20 seats = Rp5.0jt cash in, minus Rp4.0jt labor = +Rp1.0jt
Return on your time
Full house at Rp250k: +Rp1.0jt over Rp4.0jt imputed labor = a 25% labor-inclusive return, before fees, tax and refunds. Cash ROI is undefined when cash in is near zero
Cohort two onward
Prep drops to ~2h reusing material, ~12h total: break-even falls to 12 seats at Rp200k, and 15 seats turns +Rp600k

Rp 0

5. Deposit-funded scoped service

The offer
A fixed-scope deliverable, for example: a sellable offer packaged with page and payment in one week
Price to test
Rp1.5-3jt per project, 50% deposit up front (assumption)
Your time
6-10h delivery = Rp1.2-2.0jt of imputed labor
Economic break-even
Rp1.5jt works only if delivery stays under 7.5h; at 10h it loses Rp500k. Rp3jt at 10h turns +Rp1.0jt
A deposit is not profitA deposit collected before delivery is working capital plus a refund liability - you owe the work or the money back. It funds operations, but the project only makes economic sense if the price beats the hours. Scope and refund terms must be written before taking anyone's money.

6. A paid software tool or starter kit

The offer
Build once, sell copies: for example a small Python tool or a generic starter kit, sold on Gumroad at $19
Cash to start
$0 - static page on GitHub Pages, payment and delivery via Gumroad; no server to run
Net per $19 sale
$15.75 (Gumroad) or $16.70 (Lemon Squeezy), after platform fees, before tax - about Rp285k or Rp302k
Your time
~70h upfront: 55h build, 5h docs, 10h selling = Rp14.0jt (about $774) of imputed labor; then ~3h per month of support and updates
Economic break-even
About 50 sales (Gumroad) or 47 (Lemon Squeezy) to cover the upfront time, plus about 2-3 sales per month to cover ongoing support
Scenario: 15 sales in three months
$236 cash (about Rp4.3jt), minus 79h of labor (70h upfront + 9h support = Rp15.8jt) = -Rp11.5jt; it pays back only if copies keep selling without much new work

7. A small subscription tool (micro-SaaS)

The offer
A niche $5 per month utility. Revenue recurs with much less work per subscriber; likely the slowest of the seven to pay back (hypothesis - there is no demand data)
Cash to start
$0 at low usage on free hosting tiers (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, Vercel); usage limits are unverified - if the tool grows past them, real hosting costs appear
Net per subscriber per month
About $3.56 (about Rp64k) after Gumroad fees, before tax
Your time
~80h upfront = Rp16.0jt of imputed labor, plus ~8h per month to keep it running = Rp1.6jt per month, ongoing
Economic break-even
Monthly contribution = Rp64k x subscribers - Rp1.6jt ongoing labor. Below 25 subscribers it loses money every month, and at exactly 25 the upfront Rp16.0jt is never recovered. Payback of the upfront time: ~50 months at 30 subscribers, ~10 at 50, ~4 at 100
Scenario: 3, 6, then 10 subscribers over months 1-3
Rp1.2jt total cash (19 subscriber-months), minus 104h of labor (80h upfront + 24h running = Rp20.8jt) = -Rp19.6jt

What near-zero cash really means

  • Time is the real capital: roughly 15 to 80 hours before the first rupiah depending on the model, valued at Rp200k per hour purely as a decision rule. Selling hours starts cheapest; products cost the most upfront.
  • Near-zero cash assumes equipment you already own: a working laptop, internet, and free-tier accounts (GitHub Pages, Meet, Forms). Replace any of those and real costs appear.
  • A relevant developer or creator community could be an initial audience hypothesis. Permission and community norms still apply; no outreach is part of this proposal.
  • Money only appears when you choose it: a domain listed at about $8-16 per year when checked (the real price is only fixed at checkout), and payout accounts (Payoneer or PayPal) once buyers come from abroad.

The order to do it in

  1. 1Hypothesis, untested: pre-selling workshop number one to your network - 10-20 seats at Rp200k-250k, run within two weeks - is the cheapest quick demand test, because pre-sales measure real payment intent before anything gets built.
  2. 2If you would rather build than teach: start with the $15 playbook or the $19 tool or kit - the smallest products with a sellable copy - not a subscription app, which looks like the slowest payback of the seven (hypothesis, based on its arithmetic above).
  3. 3Either way, the first version goes out before it is perfect, and the demand answer comes from real payments, not from building more.
  4. 4Mentoring, community and deposit-funded service stay as later add-ons once something shows people pay you.
  5. 5Domain timing is your call - my recommendation is to wait for first revenue, not a decision made for you. Historical former-name example, not a Tumbuh availability check: insting.org was listed at about $8 first year, $12 renewal on Porkbun's pricing page when checked; price and availability are only fixed at checkout, and insting.com was reported parked at a premium marketplace.
