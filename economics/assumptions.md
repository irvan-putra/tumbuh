# Assumptions and rounding

These are historical hypothetical models, not forecasts, present-day fee advice or observed results. The original input date is 24 Oct 2026; this publication adaptation is dated 17 Nov 2026. No model is selected.

- Time value: Rp200,000/hour, an illustrative decision rule.
- Conversion: Rp18,090/USD, retained as a dated input, not a current exchange quote. The linked live rate endpoints are not historical proof of that date.
- Gumroad formula used: price x (1 - 0.10 - 0.029) - $0.50 - $0.30. A $15 sale gives $12.265; the original draft used $12.26 (truncated cent). A $19 sale gives $15.749, shown as $15.75. A $5 subscription gives $3.555, shown as $3.56. Actual provider rounding can differ.
- Lemon Squeezy formula assumed: (price x (1 - 0.05 - 0.015) - $0.50) x (1 - 0.03 approximate payout cost). This gives $13.11925 at $15 (original draft used $13.11) and $16.74705 at $19 (original draft used $16.70). The $16.70 figure is a conservative approximate original input, not an exact evaluation of that formula. No fee model has been refreshed for this adaptation.
- Economic break-even counts assumed labor; cash collected is not profit. Whole-unit thresholds round up. Domain, bank-transfer, hosting, refund, tax and payout costs must be checked for an actual project.
- Charts preserve the original rounded scenario amounts. They omit scoped service because no fixed volume scenario was specified. The scenario CSV contains rounded values, not transaction data.
- The workshop copy proposes starting at 10 seats, but the first-cohort model requires 20 at Rp200k or 16 at Rp250k to cover Rp4 million of assumed labor. Ten seats at Rp200k loses Rp2 million before tax/refunds. A minimum enrollment threshold is not economic break-even.
- The source draft linked official platform pages. Those links are retained for independent checking, but their current content does not verify the historical fee claims. Current eligibility, terms, fees and payout support are unverified here.
- No individual biography, employment history, membership, contacts or personal audience context is included.
